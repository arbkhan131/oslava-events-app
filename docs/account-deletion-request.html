What to include in a request

  • Your registered phone number.
  • Your Worker ID, if you know it.
  • A short statement that you want account deletion.

What happens next

Oslava Events verifies the request, disables account access where appropriate, removes current profile PII and queues private profile-photo deletion within the approved 30-day handling period, unless a legal hold applies. Operational, audit, staffing and legal records may be retained under the published retention policy.
